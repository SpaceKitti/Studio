## Throughline

You were supposed to be temporary here — an engineering intern with a kit-table mind, not a tower full of answers. Then the city failed in pieces: virus panic, cascading infrastructure, people running for exits that might not exist. Your partner was already gone on a trip when it started. You stay. The balcony becomes a vertical life. Ember finds you. You make do.

Months later, someone you love tries to come home to a building that is no longer the same home — and to a you who has learned survival the hard way.

**Mood (locked with Prism):** hopeful scrappy DIY, golden-hour / neon dusk — not grimdark. Soft panic → quiet. Cat as warmth and agency.

## Milestone story map (narrative only)

### M0 — Quiet after the crowd *(buildable now)*
**Play:** two balconies, Ember, kg, containers, plants. Interior blocked.

**Story spine (H0–H2):**
1. **Stay** — Soft chaos downstairs/upstairs fades. You don’t run.
2. **Cross** — Jump to neighbor balcony. Garden + drawers. Theme: finish what they started.
3. **Glass** — Ember paws the **neighbor** door. You open. You adopt. Home glass stays your blocked apartment.

**Optional bark / journal (cheap, high flavor):**
- Sticky note on door frame: trip dates, “back Sunday,” already wrong.
- Neighbor note by empty bowl: “Please feed —” name torn off.
- Intern’s own half-message: “I’ll wait here. Don’t take the tunnels.”

No partner on-screen in M0. Absence only.

### M1 — Interior + desk life *(later)*
Door opens into the apartment. Real desk, water stores, first “fill everything” memory if water already failed. Crafting UI starts to matter. Partner still absent — but their stuff is in the apartment (bag, jacket, charger, a half-packed suitcase that never left).

**Theme:** home was always shared; you’re keeping the light on.

### M2 — Vertical stretch *(later)*
Fire escape becomes usable as **map/node** travel (hardware, groceries). Solar kit fantasy (3 shards + battery). Ember brings pigeons on a timer. Furniture bridges.

**Theme:** the tower is a machine you can jury-rig.

### M3 — Return *(speculative arc — Akitti seed 2026-09-08)*
**Partner** was away on a trip when collapse hit. In-world clock: on the order of **a couple of months** before they reach the building again (exact timing tunable).

**Why it hurts (good drama, not misery porn):**
- You built a survivable balcony life for *one* — then for Ember — without knowing if they were alive.
- They arrive expecting the old apartment routine; you have rituals, weight rules, water discipline, a cat that isn’t theirs-but-is.
- Neither of you is wrong. Both have to renegotiate “home.”

**Return beat options (pick later, don’t implement yet):**
- A. They appear on the **street below / lobby node** and you guide them up the fire escape (first co-op climb fantasy).
- B. They make it to the **neighbor stack** and shout across; you bridge.
- C. Ember reacts first — sound / run to door — before you see them.

**Speculative flags:** partner name, job, how they survived the trip city, whether they bring news of infected zones, whether they’re hurt or healthy. **Not locked.**
