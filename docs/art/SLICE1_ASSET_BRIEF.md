## Lighting (do this first — biggest mood win)

1. **Key:** soft daylight from sky / open balcony — warm, slightly high (afternoon, not noon harsh).
2. **Fill:** cooler, weaker bounce from city haze beyond the railing.
3. **Warmth anchor:** amber **desk lamp** indoors — spill should read through / at the glass from the balcony side.
4. Avoid: uniform grey ambient, cyberpunk cyan fill, rain-forever mood.

Target read: “failed city out there, your place still yours.”

---

## Desk (interior)

**Done when:** reads as intern engineering desk, not lab / bunker / grey cube.

| Element | Placeholder direction |
|---------|----------------------|
| Desk body | Warm wood OR scratched pale laminate — not pure grey |
| Lamp | Small amber point / spot (the warmth anchor) |
| Clutter (3–5 props max) | Multimeter-ish brick, cable coil, screwdriver, coffee mug, sticky-note stack |
| Materials | Copper/zip-tie accents OK; no military OD green |
| Scale | Human reach — tools readable from player standing at desk |

Skip: holographic screens, weapon racks, pristine Apple-store desk.

## Pots / plants

### Player balcony — herbs
- 2–3 small pots, **herb green** foliage punch
- Terracotta or sun-bleached plastic rims
- Cluster near railing / wall — lived-in, not garden-center neat

### Neighbor — potato / tomato
- Larger soil bed / grow bags OR 2 big pots
- Tomato: one plant with a bit of fruit color pop
- Potato: lower green, unfinished (soil bag / stake nearby)
- Read: “someone started, you can finish”

### Shared
- Soil = warm brown, not asphalt black
- One thriving + one struggling plant if you only have time for two

## Surfaces (anti-grey-box)

| Surface | Tint |
|---------|------|
| Floor interior | Warm linoleum / wood plank — not concrete grey |
| Walls interior | Off-white / warm plaster, slight scuff |
| Balcony slab | Warm concrete / sun-bleached |
| Railings | Scratched aluminum or painted metal (soft, not black void) |
| Glass | Cooler, slight sky reflection; amber spill from inside |

City beyond: cooler desaturated haze — failure stays *out there*.
