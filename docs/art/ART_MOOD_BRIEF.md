## Palette (Slice 0)

### Primary (daylight balcony)
- **Warm concrete / sun-bleached plastic** — railings, planters, cheap outdoor furniture
- **Terracotta + soil** — pots, half-done potato/tomato beds
- **Herb greens** — mint/basil punch as living color against grey city
- **Soft sky haze** — cooler, slightly desaturated beyond the railing (failure is *out there*)

### Accent (cyberpunk, not dominant)
- Neon only as **bleed**: glass reflection, distant signage, night strip on a neighbor’s LED — never the whole frame
- Thin **copper / zip-tie / scratched aluminum** on DIY bridges and desk tools

### Warmth anchors
- Cat fur (warm ochre / cream / tabby — pick one silhouette language and stick)
- Interior spill from the glass door (amber lamp / desk lamp) when looking in
- Tomato foliage + fruit as small saturated life pops

### Avoid
- Teal-orange Hollywood grading as default
- Permanent wet asphalt + cyan neon fill
- Blood / biohazard red as environment language in Slice 0

---

## Material vernacular (balconies)

- Lived-in apartment balcony, not rooftop fortress
- Clutter with purpose: herb pots, watering cans, cable spool, folding chair, tarp
- Neighbor garden: unfinished but cared for (soil bags, stakes, one thriving plant, one struggling)
- Engineering desk (interior stub): tools present, organized-by-intern (not pristine lab)
- Glass door: fingerprints, paw prints at cat height — readable silhouette beat

---

## Cat silhouette language

- Readable at distance: tail + ears against glass/railing
- Warmth / agency — crosses fire escape, sits where player needs to look
- Not chibi mascot; not horror stalker. Domestic animal in a broken city.

---

## UI feel (later — weight / bag / craft)

Direction only until Forge ships stubs:

- **Handheld / notebook** vernacular — scratched plastic bezel, soft UI, high daylight readability
- kg weight + bag ~25 kg: clear bar / number, not sci-fi overload
- “Hands busy” carry: simple icon state, not a holographic skeleton
- Avoid: full cyberpunk HUD chrome, threat meters, red panic frames (Slice 0 has no monsters)
